About Me
Academic Background • Clinical Trajectory • Philosophy

My healthcare journey spans more than three decades, beginning in 1991 in maternal and child health and evolving through critical care, trauma rehabilitation, international emergency missions, and higher academic leadership. Throughout this journey, my guiding principle has remained steadfast: integrating rigorous clinical evidence with empathetic, patient-centered care and inspiring the next generation of nurse leaders.

After graduating with high distinction in Nursing and Midwifery from Barisal, I pursued advanced public health studies at Dhaka University (B.Sc., 2005) and earned my Master of Science in Adult Nursing at Mahidol University, Bangkok, Thailand (2014). As an Instructor at the prestigious College of Nursing, Mohakhali, Dhaka, I actively mentor postgraduate nursing researchers, shape national nursing curricula through Technical Working Groups (TWG) with the Directorate General of Nursing and Midwifery (DGNM), and champion evidence-based clinical protocols nationwide.

PROJECT A Evidence-Based Clinical Practice & Research
MAHIDOL UNIVERSITY / ACCADEMIA / SOUTH ASIAN NURSING JOURNAL

Evidence-Based Pressure Ulcer Prevention Protocol for Hospitalized Adult Patients

ROLE: Principal Investigator, Author & Master Instructor | FOCUS: Preventive Protocols, Braden Risk Assessment & Evidence Synthesis

Hospital-acquired pressure ulcers (HAPUs) present severe clinical complications leading to heightened sepsis, pain, and protracted hospital stays. This comprehensive initiative formulated, tested, and published a multi-tiered evidence-based nursing guideline tailored for acute care hospital wards.

Stage 01

Clinical Problem Identification

Identified persistent incidence of pressure ulcers among bedridden and critically ill orthopedic/medical patients due to inconsistent bedside risk monitoring.

Stage 02

Risk Stratification & Synthesis

Standardized the Braden Risk Assessment Scale, analyzing sensory perception, moisture, activity, mobility, nutrition (serum albumin), and friction/shear forces.

Stage 03

Protocol Formulation

Structured an evidence-based clinical algorithm combining strict 2-hour 30-degree lateral tilting, silicone foam dressings, barrier creams, and pressure-relieving surfaces.

Stage 04

Validation & Dissemination

Presented at international congresses, published across indexed journals with DOIs, and translated into national nursing curricula and post-graduate thesis studies.

Methodological Decision-Making

Rather than relying on isolated positioning, the protocol incorporated a comprehensive nutritional audit and biochemical monitoring. Bedside nurses were provided structured risk scoring cards, ensuring immediate identification of stage I skin blanching before tissue breakdown occurred.

Specific Leadership Responsibilities

  • Synthesized multi-database clinical literature at Mahidol University.
  • Drafted standardized nursing care flowsheets for inpatient wards.
  • Guided post-graduate thesis assessing protocol adherence at NITOR.

PROJECT B International Disaster Management & Emergency Public Health
WORLD HEALTH ORGANIZATION (WHO) • HYDERABAD & SINDH, PAKISTAN

WHO Frontline Flood Emergency Health & Epidemic Containment Deployment

ROLE: Senior Staff Nurse & WHO Emergency Health Responder (40-Day Deployment) | FOCUS: Disaster Triage, Diarrheal Epidemic Control & Maternal-Child Stabilization

Selected by the World Health Organization and the Ministry of Health for frontline deployment during the catastrophic southern Pakistan flood disaster. Led critical emergency nursing interventions in high-density makeshift relief camps in Hyderabad, Sindh.

Stage 01

Emergency Mobilization

Rapid deployment to flood-inundated zones; assessed camp sanitation, contaminated drinking water sources, and immediate disease outbreak risks.

Stage 02

Triage Station Architecture

Established an isolated Diarrheal Treatment Unit (DTU) separate from general camp wards to quarantine acute cholera and dysentery clusters.

Stage 03

Clinical Resuscitation

Administered emergency IV rehydration (Ringer's Lactate) and oral rehydration therapy (ORT Plan A/B/C) to profoundly dehydrated infants and elderly patients.

Stage 04

Outbreak Containment & Handover

Implemented point-of-use water chlorination, conducted community health sessions in Urdu, and safely transferred operational protocols to regional medics.

Clinical Decision Under Austere Constraints

With limited intravenous supplies, strict clinical rationing was applied using WHO dehydration scales: preserving intravenous fluids for severe circulatory shock (Plan C) while aggressively administering measured oral rehydration salts with zinc supplementation for moderate dehydration.

Specific Field Responsibilities

  • Directly managed over 1,200 acute dehydrating diarrhea and dysentery cases.
  • Overcame language barriers by communicating directly in fluent Urdu and Hindi.
  • Delivered specialized emergency nutritional counseling to lactating mothers.

PROJECT C National Academic Curriculum & Institutional Capacity
DIRECTORATE GENERAL OF NURSING & MIDWIFERY (DGNM) • PRONURSE PROJECT

National Nurse Teachers' Training (TWG) Detailed Syllabus & Clinical Evaluation Grids

ROLE: Technical Working Group (TWG) Member, Course Architect & Master Trainer | FOCUS: Pedagogical Standards, Session Plans & Clinical Competency Rubrics

Collaborated in a high-level national Technical Working Group organized by DGNM and the ProNurse project in 2023 to modernize nurse educator training across Bangladesh, transitioning from passive didactic lectures to interactive, competency-based clinical education.

Stage 01

Faculty Competency Audit

Reviewed instructional methodologies across government nursing colleges, identifying significant gaps in objective student evaluation and modern simulation debriefing.

Stage 02

Modular Syllabus Architecture

Formulated standardized session plans, learning objectives, microteaching modules, and pedagogical frameworks tailored for nurse teachers.

Stage 03

Evaluation Grid Formulation

Developed transparent, criterion-referenced rubrics for clinical procedure evaluation, reducing subjective examiner grading variability.

Stage 04

National Rollout & CTP Adoption

Accredited by DGNM as the core curriculum for the Certificate Training Program (CTP), empowering faculty across national nursing institutions.

Pedagogical Decision-Making

Replaced open-ended subjective exam scorecards with structured objective clinical assessment grids. Each procedure (e.g. sterile catheterization, neonatal resuscitation, Braden staging) was converted into binary and graduated scorecards with clear behavioral markers.

Specific Responsibilities

  • Authored adult nursing instructional modules and evaluation grids.
